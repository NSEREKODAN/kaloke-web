Our Story

Building Uganda's Future, One Student at a Time

Founded in 1995, Kaloke Christian High School has grown to become one of Uganda's leading secondary schools. Registered with the Ministry of Education and Sports and licensed as a UNEB examination centre, we pride ourselves in academic excellence, discipline, and holistic student development.

We serve both day and boarding students from across Uganda, offering the national curriculum at O-Level (S1–S4) and A-Level (S5–S6). Our alumni have gone on to excel in universities across Uganda and abroad.

Our Mission

To provide quality, holistic secondary education that nurtures disciplined, skilled, and God-fearing citizens ready to contribute to national development.

Our Vision

To be a leading centre of academic excellence in Uganda, producing well-rounded graduates who excel in higher education and life.

Our Core Values

Discipline · Integrity · Excellence · Respect · Service · Faith
