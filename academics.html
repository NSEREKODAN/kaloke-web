Uganda National Curriculum

Our Academic Programs

We follow the Uganda national curriculum as examined by the Uganda National Examinations Board (UNEB).

Ordinary Level (S1 – S4)

Students pursue the Uganda Certificate of Education (UCE) curriculum, which comprises compulsory subjects and electives.

Compulsory Subjects

  • English Language
  • Mathematics
  • Physics
  • Chemistry
  • Biology
  • History & Political Education
  • Geography
  • Christian Religious Education
  • Entrepreneurship Education
  • ICT

Electives

  • Literature in English
  • Luganda
  • Kiswahili
  • Fine Art
  • Music
  • Agriculture
  • Physical Education

Advanced Level (S5 – S6)

The Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education (UACE) program offers diverse subject combinations to prepare students for university and careers.

Popular Combinations

  • PEM (Physics, Economics, Mathematics)
  • PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)
  • PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • HEG (History, Economics, Geography)
  • HEL (History, Economics, Literature)
  • HED (History, Economics, Divinity)
  • BLE (Biology, Literature, Economics)
  • Subsidiary: General Paper & Computer Science

Co-Curricular Activities

We believe in holistic education. Every student participates in at least one co-curricular activity.

  • Football (Boys & Girls)
  • Volleyball
  • Basketball
  • Athletics
  • Music, Dance & Drama (MDD)
  • Debate & Public Speaking
  • Scouts & Girl Guides
  • Christian Union & Scripture Union
  • Wildlife Clubs
  • Entrepreneurship Club
