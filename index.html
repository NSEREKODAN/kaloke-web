About Our School

A Legacy of Academic Excellence in Uganda

Mulusa High School is a registered secondary school under the Ministry of Education and Sports, Uganda. We offer both O-Level (UCE) and A-Level (UACE) curricula, preparing students for national examinations and life beyond.

Our holistic approach combines strong academics, co-curricular activities, and Christian values to shape well-rounded citizens ready to serve Uganda and the world.
