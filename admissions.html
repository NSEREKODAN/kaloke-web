Admission Process 2025

We welcome applications for Senior One (S1) and Senior Five (S5) as well as transfers for other classes.

1

Obtain Application Form

Pick a form at the school office or download from our website. Fee: UGX 20,000.

2

Submit Documents

Submit completed form with PLE/UCE results slip, passport photos, and birth certificate copy.

3

Interview & Placement

Shortlisted candidates are invited for an interview and placement test.

4

Pay Fees & Report

Pay the required fees and report on the official opening date.

Admission Requirements

  • PLE Results Slip (for S1 applicants)
  • UCE Results Slip (for S5 applicants)
  • Photocopy of Birth Certificate
  • Four recent passport-size photos
  • Recommendation letter from previous school
  • Completed application form
